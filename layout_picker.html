Layout coordinate picker

Card canvas is fixed at 1500 × 2100 pixels.
Origin (0, 0) is the top-left corner.

Each LAYOUT box is:
(left, top, right, bottom)

  • left = X of left edge
  • top = Y of top edge
  • right = X of right edge
  • bottom = Y of bottom edge

Move a box right → increase left and right.
Move a box down → increase top and bottom.
Make wider → increase right (or decrease left).
